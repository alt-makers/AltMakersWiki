## Safety

- **Disc: only use the downward-moving half** (the side where the disc spins down toward the table). On the other half the disc lifts the work and throws it.
- **Keep the gap between table and belt or disc small** (2 mm or less). Small pieces and fingertips get pulled into a big gap.
- **Hold small parts with a clamp or a jig.** A sander removes skin as easily as wood.
- **Don't sand metal** on a sander that's full of wood dust. Sparks plus dust can start a fire in the extraction.

## Rules

- Induction by a shop lead before your first use.
- Dust extraction on.
- Report worn or torn belts and discs.

## How to use

1. Check the belt or disc isn't torn and the table gap is small.
2. Extraction on, then switch on.
3. Press the work lightly against the belt or disc and keep it moving so you don't burn a groove into it.
4. Switch off and wait for it to stop before you leave.

## Tips

- Use the tilting table and the mitre gauge to sand precise angles.
- Burn marks mean you're pressing too hard or the belt is clogged. A belt-cleaning stick (crepe rubber) restores a clogged belt.
